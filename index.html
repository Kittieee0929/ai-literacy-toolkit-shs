ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Learn to use AI wisely.

This AI Literacy Toolkit is a needs-based educational resource developed for Senior High School students in Laoag City, Ilocos Norte.

The toolkit focuses on seven essential areas of AI literacy and aims to support students in becoming more informed, critical, ethical, and responsible users of artificial intelligence for learning.

Research Project

Developing and Validating a Needs-Based AI Literacy Toolkit for Senior High School Students in Laoag City, Ilocos Norte
