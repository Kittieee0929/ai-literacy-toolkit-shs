04 / ABOUT THE STUDY

Designed for students.
Built from identified needs.

This AI Literacy Toolkit is a needs-based educational resource developed for Senior High School students in Laoag City, Ilocos Norte.

It focuses on seven essential areas of AI literacy and supports students in becoming more informed, critical, ethical, and responsible users of artificial intelligence for learning.
